CORE VALUE PROPOSITION

Boundary Perpetual Contracts deliver defined-risk, transparent trading

Bounded Risk

Maximum loss is capped at your stake. No liquidation mechanics, no surprise losses. Know your risk before you trade.

Fixed Payoffs

Payout amount locked when you open the position. No slippage at settlement. Complete price transparency from entry to exit.

Flexible Expression

Trade directional views within defined boundaries. No need to predict exact prices or timing. Just express your market view.

On-Chain Settlement

Decentralized execution via smart contracts. Transparent, immutable settlement. HyperEVM infrastructure for fast, reliable processing.

Early Exit Flexibility

Buyback mechanism allows you to exit early with updated market quotes. Maintain control of your position at all times.

Perfect for Risk-Averse Traders

Ideal alternative to liquidation-based perpetuals. Appeals to traders seeking structured, defined-outcome derivatives.
